Join us for our November event as we kick off our local tree-growing project!

We’ll be meeting at [Eaves Playground](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=53.745728,-2.027694) at 10:00 to collect and pot up native Oak seedlings. Our goal is to nurture these young trees so they’re ready for future planting right here in Colden Clough.

<!--more-->

How you can help:

- Bring along: A trowel and any spare small plant pots if you have them.🪴
- Take some home: If everyone takes just 5 to 10 potted seedlings home to water and look after for a year or two, we’ll have a strong, locally grown batch of native oaks ready to plant back into the Clough!🌱

Many hands (and green thumbs) make light work—come along, bring a friend, and help us grow our local canopy!
