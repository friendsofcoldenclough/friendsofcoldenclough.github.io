**Connecting with nature and each other in Colden Clough**.<br>
Join us for a special guided walk exploring the rich ecology of the Clough, with local ecologist Miranda Cowan.

<!--more-->

Together we’ll: 

* take time to notice the changing woodland
* discover how the cooler, wetter weather effects the ecology of the Clough
* reflect on how the area has changed and what its future could be

Meet at [Eaves Playground](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=53.745728,-2.027694) at 10:00 - suitable for all, just bring sturdy footwear and curiosity! The walk will take two to two and a half hours.

Whether you’re interested in ecology, or simply enjoy a good walk, we’d love to have you along!
